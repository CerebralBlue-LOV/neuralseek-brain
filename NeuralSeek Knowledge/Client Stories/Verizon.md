# Verizon

## Background
As a publicly traded company, Verizon regularly engages with shareholders, analysts, and the media, particularly around earnings announcements and corporate updates.

## Key Problem
Earnings calls and investor Q&A sessions are high-stakes events where executives must respond quickly and confidently to challenging questions. Anticipating these questions and preparing precise, data-backed responses is a time-intensive process, often requiring coordination across finance, investor relations, and corporate communications teams. Delays or incomplete preparation can lead to missed messaging opportunities or inconsistent information delivery.

## Solution
Verizon deployed NeuralSeek AI Agents to automate the process of anticipating, refining, and preparing responses for shareholder and analyst questions. The system proactively reviews upcoming earnings reports, press releases, and internal briefing materials to forecast likely questions. It then cross-references these with historical Q&A archives, call transcripts, and investor behavior patterns to refine predictions. Finally, NeuralSeek generates context-rich draft responses, complete with relevant financial metrics, operational data, and market context, ready for executive review and delivery.

Outcomes:
- Improved readiness for high-pressure investor/media interactions
- Increased accuracy of responses by grounding via verifiable data
- Reduced preparation time for earnings calls and other disclosures

NeuralSeek has become a foundation for strategic expansion, as Verizon is now leveraging NeuralSeek for extensive enterprise-wide search powered by Agentic AI, enabling employees across the organization to find and act on critical information faster and more intelligently.

## App Integrations
- Earnings report and press release repositories
- Internal briefing material stores
- Historical Q&A archives and earnings call transcripts
- Financial and operational data systems (for grounded, data-backed responses)
