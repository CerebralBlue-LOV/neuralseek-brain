# Clip

## Background
Clip is a leading Mexican fintech company specializing in digital payments and commerce platforms. As Mexico's first "payments unicorn," Clip enables businesses of all sizes to accept digital payments, driving financial inclusion in a market where 38% of adults remain unbanked yet most own mobile phones. Clip serves hundreds of thousands of businesses, many new to financial services.

## Key Problem
Clip generates frequent inquiries about fees, account setup, payment options, and credit lines. Managing thousands of new customers each month without a traditional banking infrastructure required a scalable, accurate, and fast support solution.

## Solution
Since 2022, NeuralSeek has powered Clip's advanced AI virtual assistant using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) technology. The assistant identifies customer intent, retrieves real-time data from external databases, and delivers context-rich answers instantly. Leveraging NLP, the assistant can recognize when customers reference available products and, if a request is vague, guide them with targeted suggestions until the most accurate answer is delivered.

Outcomes:
- 6,000+ simultaneous interactions handled
- 66%+ of inquiries resolved autonomously with generative AI
- 80% faster interactions vs. call centers
- 28% annual growth in chatbot usage

The solution delivers a 24/7 omnichannel experience, frees agents for complex cases, and accelerates financial inclusion for thousands of entrepreneurs across Mexico.
