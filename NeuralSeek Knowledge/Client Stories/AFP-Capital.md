# AFP Capital

## Background
AFP Capital, a Chilean pension fund administrator owned by the Colombian financial group SURA, serves millions of clients across the country. The organization is responsible for managing retirement savings, providing financial guidance, and ensuring timely, accurate communication with both customers and internal teams.

## Key Problem
AFP Capital needed a way to deliver consistent, high-quality responses across multiple customer and employee interaction channels, including IVR, WhatsApp, the company's main website, and internal support platforms. Fragmented knowledge sources and manual response processes risked slowing service times, creating inconsistencies, and placing additional strain on customer service teams.

## Solution
AFP Capital implemented NeuralSeek as a unified AI-powered response engine, integrating it into IVR systems, WhatsApp, the corporate website, and internal platforms. NeuralSeek delivers fast, accurate, and policy-compliant answers, drawing from verified knowledge sources to ensure consistency. The platform's multi-channel deployment ensures both clients and employees receive the same high-quality information, regardless of the channel used, while built-in guardrails maintain compliance with regulatory requirements.

Outcomes:
- Delivered consistent, accurate information across all customer and employee touchpoints
- Improved response times and customer satisfaction through automation
- Reduced workload on support teams by deflecting routine inquiries
- Ensured compliance with regulatory standards via NeuralSeek's governance and guardrails

## App Integrations
- IVR systems (voice channel)
- WhatsApp (messaging channel)
- Corporate website
- Internal support platforms
- Verified internal knowledge sources
