# Snap Inc.

## Background
Snap Inc. is a global technology company that empowers people to express themselves, live in the moment, and learn about the world. With a distributed workforce spanning multiple regions, Snap required a centralized, instant-access solution to streamline HR information delivery, reduce support overhead, and improve the employee experience.

## Key Problem
Finding accurate HR information at Snap was time-consuming, with scattered resources and delays in responses from support teams. Managers and employees struggled to quickly access details on People programs, policies, and processes, leading to inefficiencies and reduced productivity. Support staff spent disproportionate time on repetitive inquiries instead of higher-value initiatives.

## Solution
NeuralSeek powers "Chilla," Snap's internal Slack bot that acts as a secure, AI-driven HR assistant. Using Slack as the front-end and NeuralSeek as the back-end, Chilla functions as a ChatGPT-like experience with strict AI guardrails to ensure compliance with internal policy standards. The assistant integrates with multiple knowledge sources, including Snappy pages and pre-defined Q&A datasets, with contextual awareness by country and city. It supports advanced workflows such as:

- Providing instant HR and policy information
- Nudging managers to approve time off requests and complete onboarding tasks
- Reminding managers about direct report milestones (birthdays, anniversaries)
- Surfacing new hire checklists
- Accessing Workday data to provide team member information
- Generating prompts for council meetings
- Creating Zendesk tickets for escalations
- Capturing feedback to continuously improve responses

NeuralSeek has become Snap's single source of truth for HR knowledge, ensuring consistent policy adherence and delivering an always-on, compliant, and context-aware HR support experience.

Outcomes:
- Reduced HR response times from hours to seconds
- Improved employee satisfaction with immediate, accurate answers
- Significant reduction in repetitive support queries, freeing HR teams
- Global scalability with instant availability to employees via Slack

## App Integrations
- Slack (front-end channel for employees and managers)
- Snappy pages (internal knowledge source)
- Workday (HRIS / team member data)
- Zendesk (escalation ticket creation)
- Pre-defined Q&A datasets
