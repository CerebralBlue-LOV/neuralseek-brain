# Adobe

## Background
Adobe continually innovates to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and maintain governance standards across its global operations.

## Key Problem
Adobe's HR teams were spending significant time managing high-volume inbox traffic, fielding repetitive questions, and manually routing inquiries to the appropriate teams. Additionally, knowledge scattered across emails, messages, and documents slowed project momentum and made it difficult for employees to quickly find critical information. The company needed a scalable, governance-driven AI solution to automate repetitive tasks while ensuring compliance and maintaining control.

## Solution
Adobe deployed NeuralSeek to power automated HR inbox triage, instantly answering common queries and routing complex cases to the correct HR team. NeuralSeek's unified knowledge access capabilities connect emails, messages, and documents, enabling rapid retrieval of relevant information without manual search. Built-in permissions, audit tracking, and AI guardrails ensure all outputs meet Adobe's governance standards.

Outcomes:
- Reduced HR workload through automated triage of common inquiries
- Accelerated information retrieval and project momentum across departments
- Governance, compliance, and trust through built-in guardrails and audit logs
- Enabled scalable AI innovation by combining business-user autonomy with IT oversight in a Center of Excellence model

Following the success of these initial deployments, Adobe is expanding NeuralSeek as the backbone of a Center of Excellence for Agentic AI, empowering business users to create AI Agents in a true no-code environment, while IT governs the process via a hub-and-spoke model.

## App Integrations
- Email systems (HR inbox triage and routing)
- Messaging platforms
- Document repositories (unified knowledge access across emails, messages, and documents)
