# Banco de la República Oriental del Uruguay (BROU)

## Background
Banco de la República Oriental del Uruguay (BROU) is the largest and oldest bank in Uruguay, serving over one million clients across retail, commercial, and corporate banking. With a vast branch network and diverse service portfolio, BROU plays a central role in the country's financial infrastructure.

## Key Problem
BROU sought to modernize its internal support systems to improve knowledge access for employees and streamline process documentation management. The bank needed a solution capable of providing fast, accurate, and context-aware answers to internal queries, with the scalability to eventually extend AI-powered services directly to its customer base.

## Solution
BROU implemented NeuralSeek to power its internal chatbot and process documentation search, enabling employees to retrieve verified information instantly. NeuralSeek's no-code interface allows BROU to adapt and expand the system quickly, while its built-in governance, guardrails, and audit logging ensure compliance with the bank's security and operational standards. The platform is designed to integrate seamlessly with future customer-facing channels, supporting the bank's roadmap for AI-driven service expansion.

Outcomes:
- Accelerated employee access to critical process and policy information
- Improved internal operational efficiency and knowledge sharing
- Established a secure, governed AI foundation for future client initiatives
- Positioned the bank to scale AI services to over one million customers

## App Integrations
- Internal process and policy documentation repositories
- Internal employee chatbot front-end
- Designed for future integration with customer-facing channels
