# AdMed, Inc.

## Background
AdMed, Inc., a U.S.-based company, specializes in developing medical education and training materials for the pharmaceutical and biotech sectors. Their content is created by highly skilled medical professionals to ensure accuracy, credibility, and impact.

## Key Problem
Creating high-quality medical training content is a labor-intensive process that requires significant subject-matter expertise and time investment. AdMed needed a way to streamline production without compromising the accuracy or depth of its materials, while also easing the workload for the doctors and medical experts involved.

## Solution
By integrating NeuralSeek's no-code AI orchestration platform into its workflow, AdMed accelerates content creation and enhances the quality of its training materials. NeuralSeek assists in research, drafting, and knowledge retrieval, allowing medical professionals to focus on higher-value tasks while maintaining strict accuracy and compliance standards.

By leveraging NeuralSeek, AdMed has reduced content creation time, enabling faster delivery to clients, while increasing productivity for medical professionals by automating repetitive tasks. The platform has also enhanced the quality and consistency of educational materials, ultimately strengthening AdMed's competitive advantage through innovative AI-enabled workflows.

- Internal research and content authoring workflows
- Curated medical knowledge sources used for retrieval and drafting
