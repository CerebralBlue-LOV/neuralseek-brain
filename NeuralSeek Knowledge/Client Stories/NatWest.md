# NatWest Group

## Background
NatWest Group is one of the UK's largest retail and commercial banks, serving millions of customers across personal, business, and corporate banking segments. As part of its ongoing digital transformation, NatWest launched a 12-week pilot for a next-generation digital assistant, "Cora+," leveraging NeuralSeek's Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) capabilities to enhance customer service through secure, responsible AI.

## Key Problem
NatWest's existing virtual assistant, Cora, could already address over 150 day-to-day banking queries. However, to meet rising customer expectations for conversational, context-aware assistance, NatWest sought to incorporate generative AI in a way that complied with strict EU GDPR and banking regulations. The challenge was to ensure accuracy, governance, and accountability in every AI-generated response for a public-facing, high-volume support channel.

## Solution
Cora+ integrates ChatGPT 3.5 Turbo as the conversational engine, with NeuralSeek's RAG orchestration layer delivering verified, knowledge-grounded answers. NeuralSeek's AI Guardrails ensure that every customer-facing response aligns with compliance requirements and NatWest's internal policies. The NeuralSeek features leveraged in this solution are:

- Granular Governance Controls — rules for banking-specific queries
- Audit Logs — complete traceability of each AI interaction for compliance
- Replay Functionality — enables post-interaction review of AI workflows
- KnowledgeBase Integration — responses sourced from approved content
- Multi-Channel Support — available on website, mobile app, and online banking

NatWest's Cora+ pilot has delivered responsible AI deployment in a GDPR-regulated environment, increasing accuracy and trust through grounded, verifiable responses. The solution has enhanced customer self-service across web, mobile, and online banking channels, while improving operational efficiency by reducing the workload on live agents handling common banking queries. With a future-ready governance framework in place, NatWest is well-positioned to scale AI capabilities across additional banking services.
