        WHAT'S INSIDE THE ENGAGEMENT                                                                     5 CASES


Use cases delivered for Children's Health.

   01   Clinician- & staff-facing chatbot
  A HIPAA-aware ChatGPT-style assistant for Children's clinicians, nurses, and administrative staff — running
  inside Children's tenant with SSO, RBAC scoped to each user's role and unit, content filtering tuned for
  pediatric care, and a full audit trail on every prompt and response.

   HIPAA-AWARE        SSO + RBAC       AUDIT TRAIL
 02    Clinical Policy & Pathway chat
Pre-loaded with Children's clinical pathways, pediatric medication protocols (including weight-based dosing),
infection-prevention standards, and regulatory guidance. Clinicians get answers with direct citations to the
source policy and paragraph, auto-refreshed the moment a policy is republished — and any response that
would expose restricted PHI is blocked before it ever reaches the user.

 CITED ANSWERS          AUTO-REFRESH       PHI GUARDRAILS




 03    OCR for referral packets & scanned charts
Outside records, faxed referral packets, handwritten intake forms, imaging reports, and legacy scanned
charts are OCR'd in-place. Extracted content feeds directly into the chat so a clinician can summarize a
referral, pull allergies or prior diagnoses, or ask follow-up questions without ever opening a separate
imaging or records tool.

 OCR       IN-CHAT       FAXES + SCANS




 04    Self-service pediatric cohort analytics
Quality, operations, and clinical research teams ask plain-English questions of Children's governed datasets
— readmission rates by service line, length of stay for a specific cohort, NICU throughput, ED wait times,
outcomes by age band. No SQL, no analyst queue; every answer shows the underlying query and sourced
data so results are auditable before they go into a committee deck.

 NATURAL-LANGUAGE          GOVERNED DATA       AUDIT-READY




 05    Family & caregiver call analytics
AI-driven insight on every inbound call from families, referring providers, scheduling, and nurse lines — not
just the small sample a QA team can listen to manually. Surfaces caregiver distress signals, booking friction,
unresolved clinical questions, and coaching moments so service and ops teams can intervene on the calls
that matter most.

 EVERY CALL          DISTRESS SIGNALS       COACHING MOMENTS
