# Penn State University

## Background
Penn State University, ranked among the top six percent of universities worldwide, serves nearly 90,000 students across multiple campuses. Known for its academic excellence, robust research programs, and commitment to student success, Penn State continually invests in innovative solutions that enhance the campus experience and foster a strong sense of community.

## Key Problem
Student leaders and internal analyses revealed a pressing need for a more efficient, personalized way to connect students with university resources. The existing channels for academic, health, wellness, and financial aid support often required navigating multiple systems, leading to delays and inconsistent experiences. Penn State sought a solution that could unify access to services, deliver fast and accurate guidance, and scale across its diverse student population.

## Solution
Penn State partnered with NeuralSeek to create MyResource, a generative AI-powered digital concierge designed to streamline student access to campus services. Leveraging NeuralSeek's no-code orchestration platform, the university integrated conversational AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and enterprise-grade guardrails. MyResource is trained on Penn State-provided data, enabling precise, context-aware responses while ensuring compliance through built-in governance features like harmful language filtering and sensitive data detection.

Outcomes:
- Centralized, personalized access to academic, health, wellness, and financial aid resources
- Faster, more accurate student support, reducing friction and improving satisfaction
- Scalable AI platform capable of serving nearly 90,000 students

## App Integrations
- Penn State-provided academic, health, wellness, and financial aid data sources
- Conversational front-end available to students across campuses
