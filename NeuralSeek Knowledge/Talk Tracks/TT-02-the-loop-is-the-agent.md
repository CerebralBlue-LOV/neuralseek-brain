# TT-2. "The Loop is the Agent" (Perceive → Plan → Act → Observe → Repeat)

## Punchline
"An agent runs the ReAct loop: it perceives its environment, reasons about a goal, selects and executes a tool, observes the result, replans if needed, and repeats until the goal is reached or declared unreachable. The loop is the agent."

## Why this lands
Four words a non-technical person can hold in their head. It demystifies "agentic AI."

## NeuralSeek tie-in
Every iteration of that loop is a place to apply guardrails. Most platforms run the loop without checks. NeuralSeek puts 118 of them at every step.

## Use
Whiteboard explanation when someone asks "what even is an AI agent?"
