# TT-3. "Every major computing paradigm looked like a feature before it looked like infrastructure"

## Punchline
"The PC looked like a productivity tool. The internet looked like a publishing medium. The smartphone looked like a better phone. Cloud looked like cheaper servers. Today, AI agents look like a smarter chatbot. However, they are emerging as a new software substrate that will run workflows, services, and human-machine collaboration."

## Why this lands
Reframes the entire AI conversation from "is this a fad?" to "where are we in a 20-year cycle?" Investor-grade framing.

## NeuralSeek tie-in
"And we're the substrate for the regulated half of the economy."

## Use
Conference keynote opener. LinkedIn post lead. Board-level conversations about why AI investment is structural, not cyclical.
