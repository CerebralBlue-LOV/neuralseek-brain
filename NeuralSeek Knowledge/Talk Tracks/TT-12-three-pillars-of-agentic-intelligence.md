# TT-12. "The Three Pillars of Agentic Intelligence" (Reasoning, Memory, Knowledge)

## Category
Explaining how NeuralSeek works to non-technical people

## Punchline
Agents visualized as a Greek temple with three pillars sitting on a model layer:

- **Reasoning** (powered by MoE — Mixture of Experts)
- **Memory** (powered by MLA — Multi-head Latent Attention / KV cache)
- **Knowledge** (powered by RAG and GraphRAG)

## Why this lands
Visual, memorable, and there's a beautiful brain analogy: "The original LLMs were one giant network. Every part of the model fired for every question. Asking it the time used the same full engine as asking it to write code. The fix borrows from neuroscience. The human brain runs different regions for different tasks."

## NeuralSeek tie-in
This maps directly to NeuralSeek's category-routing capability — Mark's existing pitch about "when someone asks me about my weekend, my brain routes to my personal-life bucket. When someone asks about NeuralSeek, it's a completely different part of my brain" (07-product-talking-points.md, "Knowledge Base Architecture" section). NeuralSeek does at the workflow level what MoE does at the model level.

## Use
Whiteboard explanation when someone asks how NeuralSeek's routing works.
