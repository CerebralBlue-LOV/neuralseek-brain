# TT-1. "From Calculator to Strategist" (the cleanest mental model)

## Punchline
"A chatbot is like a calculator. Prompt in, response out. It's probabilistic, treats every request as a fresh start, and is bounded by design. An agent is like a strategist. It sets a goal, picks and uses tools, observes the result, revises the plan, and iterates until the goal is reached or declared unreachable. The difference between a chatbot and an agent is this loop."

## Why this lands
A non-technical executive instantly gets the difference. Calculator and strategist are both familiar; the gap between them is intuitive.

## NeuralSeek tie-in
This is the cleanest possible setup for the next move — "and the strategist needs guardrails the calculator never needed, which is what we built."

## Use
Opener with any non-technical board member, CFO, or new prospect on a discovery call.
