> **Solution Brief**

**<u>NeuralSeek + IBM Fusion</u>**

**Secure, No‑Code AI for Regulated Enterprises**

**<u>Executive Summary</u>**

Enterprises in regulated industries face a fundamental barrier to AI
adoption: **public‑cloud LLMs and SaaS AI agents cannot guarantee data
sovereignty, strict access control, or compliance with frameworks such
as HIPAA, PCI‑DSS, FINRA, GDPR, or CJIS**. Sending prompts or documents
to external endpoints risks exposure of sensitive information, while
multi‑tenant architectures introduce unacceptable uncertainty around
data retention, cross‑customer bleed, and auditability.

**NeuralSeek + IBM Fusion** solves this problem by delivering a fully
governed, behind‑the‑firewall AI platform that transforms private
enterprise data into production‑ready applications—without exposing any
information to external networks. NeuralSeek provides deterministic,
source‑grounded responses, while IBM Fusion enforces policy‑driven
orchestration, identity, and governance. Together, they unlock AI
productivity gains while preserving complete control over data,
workflows, and compliance.

**<u>The Problem: AI Adoption in Regulated Industries</u>**

Regulated organizations lack a unified, no‑code way to operationalize AI
using their private data. Key challenges include:

- **Data sovereignty risks** from public‑cloud LLMs

- **Inability to enforce zero‑trust controls** across external AI
  services

- **Lack of auditability and traceability** in model behavior

- **Hallucinations and non‑deterministic outputs**

- **Vendor lock‑in and retention ambiguity**

- **Compliance exposure** across HIPAA, PCI, FINRA, GDPR, CJIS, SOX,
  ITAR, and more

These industries require AI systems that operate **inside their own
security perimeter**, with full governance, logging, and policy
enforcement.

**<u>What NeuralSeek Provides</u>**

NeuralSeek is an enterprise AI platform designed for safe, compliant use
of private data. It combines:

- **Retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG)** for source‑grounded answers

- **Multi‑LLM AI Agent orchestration** for performance and cost control

- **Strict governance** including RBAC, PII filtering, and audit logging

- **Deterministic, traceable outputs** with verifiable citations

- **Deployment flexibility**: cloud or fully on‑prem

NeuralSeek ensures that every answer is tied to approved internal
sources—eliminating hallucinations and enabling compliance‑ready AI.

**<u>What IBM Fusion Provides <span class="mark">(IBM to
elaborate)</span></u>**

IBM Fusion is a secure, on‑premises platform for building and deploying
AI applications without sending data to the public cloud. It delivers:

- **Secure LLM orchestration**

- **Enterprise‑grade governance and policy enforcement**

- **Deep integration with internal systems**

- **Hardware‑rooted trust and isolated compute**

- **Support for Granite and open‑source models**

Fusion keeps every token, workflow, and dataset **inside your
firewall**, ensuring full control over identity, access, and data
movement.

**<u>Joint Value Proposition: NeuralSeek + IBM Fusion</u>**

Together, NeuralSeek and IBM Fusion create a uniquely powerful
architecture for regulated industries to develop and deploy multi agent
AI workflows.

**1. Absolute Data Privacy & Sovereignty**

When deployed on Fusion, NeuralSeek ensures:

- No external API calls

- No third‑party data exposure

- No multi‑tenant cloud risk

- No external logging or retention

All retrieval, reasoning, and orchestration remain fully contained
within your infrastructure.

**2. Deterministic, Source‑Grounded Answers**

NeuralSeek ties every response to approved internal sources, delivering:

- Highly accurate, auditable outputs

- Verifiable citations

- Confidence scoring

- Hallucination reduction

This is essential for legal, clinical, financial, and operational
workflows.

**3. Enterprise‑Grade Governance**

The combined stack provides:

- Granular RBAC

- Full audit logging

- Domain‑specific content filtering

- Policy enforcement for sensitive data

- Guardrails for every agent action

AI behavior aligns with your compliance, privacy, and risk frameworks.

**4. Safe Use of Private Data Without Model Retraining**

NeuralSeek enables real‑time retrieval from internal documents and
systems **without fine‑tuning** or exposing data to model providers.

**5. Seamless Integration With Internal Systems**

Fusion enables secure connectivity to:

- Databases

- File shares

- Knowledge repositories

- Identity systems (LDAP, SSO, RBAC)

- Line‑of‑business applications

AI agents can search, reason, and act safely inside your environment.

**6. Compliance‑Ready Architecture**

The solution meets the strictest regulatory requirements:

- HIPAA

- PCI‑DSS

- FINRA

- GDPR

- CJIS

- SOX

- ITAR

NeuralSeek ensures content‑level compliance; Fusion ensures system‑level
compliance.

**7. Faster, Safer Enterprise AI Deployment**

NeuralSeek’s no‑code configuration + Fusion’s orchestration layer
delivers:

- Rapid deployment

- Minimal custom engineering

- Consistent governance across all use cases

- Lower operational risk

**<u>Conclusion</u>**

NeuralSeek + IBM Fusion is one of the few architectures capable of
meeting the full stack of requirements demanded by regulated industries:
**security, governance, accuracy, auditability, and operational
control**. NeuralSeek solves the correctness and explainability problem;
Fusion solves the data‑sovereignty and governance problem. Together,
they enable enterprises to move from prototypes to **production‑grade AI
systems that regulators, auditors, and risk committees can trust**.
