                        NeuralSeek & eDelta
                     A Strategic AI Partnership
      Bringing together decades of industry and regulatory expertise to power future-ready AI
                                             decisions.




NeuralSeek and eDelta Consulting are proud to announce a strategic partnership focused on
delivering enterprise-grade AI solutions that meet the highest standards of compliance,
governance, and operational excellence. Together, we bring decades of expertise across IT
audit, regulatory alignment, and AI deployment to support organizations operating in complex,
highly regulated sectors such as financial services, healthcare, and government.

At the core of our offering is NeuralSeek—a secure, no-code AI decisioning platform that can
be deployed entirely on-premises or within a client’s private cloud. NeuralSeek is built around
three pillars:

1. Speed: Clients can deploy and begin iterating on production-ready AI use cases in days, not
months. This agility allows business units to innovate quickly while adhering to enterprise-level
access controls and audit requirements.

2. Simplicity: NeuralSeek democratizes AI by making advanced capabilities accessible to non-
technical users. Compliance, risk, and operational teams can build and manage workflows
independently, reducing IT dependencies and enabling real-time governance.

3. Success: Long-term outcomes are built on transparency, traceability, and trust. NeuralSeek
integrates with both structured and unstructured data sources and is fully auditable, providing
the confidence needed for regulatory reviews and future expansion.

Combined with eDelta’s 25+ years of deep industry knowledge, we offer a powerful, future-
ready solution for organizations seeking to adopt AI responsibly, securely, and at scale.
