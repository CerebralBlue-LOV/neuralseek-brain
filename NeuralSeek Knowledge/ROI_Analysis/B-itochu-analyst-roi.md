# B. Itochu Analyst ROI — Defensible Cost Calculation

*Compressing three weeks of analyst work into thirteen minutes, across 500 analysts.*

## Inputs (all sourced from public benchmarks)

- **500 analysts** in production at Itochu
- **Pre-NeuralSeek time per research deliverable:** 3 analyst-weeks (two scenarios modeled below)
- **Post-NeuralSeek time per deliverable:** 13 minutes (~0.22 hours)
- **Fully-loaded US financial-analyst cost:** **$88 / hour**
  - Derived from base + bonus of $135K × 1.30 benefits/overhead load ÷ 2,000 working hours
  - Triangulated from US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Glassdoor, and Robert Half 2026 data
  - BLS June 2025 ECI validates the ~30% benefits load

## Time saved per report

| Scenario | Pre-NS effort | Post-NS effort | Saved per report | $ saved per report @ $88/hr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** (60 focused hours = 3 weeks part-time) | 60 h | 0.22 h | 59.78 h | **~$5,260** |
| **Aggressive** (120 focused hours = 3 full work-weeks) | 120 h | 0.22 h | 119.78 h | **~$10,540** |

## Annualized savings across 500 analysts

| Reports per analyst per year | Conservative model | Aggressive model |
|---|---|---|
| 2 reports | $5.3M / yr | $10.5M / yr |
| 4 reports | **$10.5M / yr** | **$21.1M / yr** |
| 8 reports | $21.1M / yr | $42.2M / yr |
